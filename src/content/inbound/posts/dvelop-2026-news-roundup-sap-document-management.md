# d.velop in 2026: Gartner recognition, a migration partnership and AI in the SAP archive

**Disclosure first:** I work with delaware as an independent business-development partner on SAP document archiving, and that work involves d.velop documents for SAP. So I have an interest here. This post sticks to what d.velop and others have published, with links, so you can judge for yourself. Most of the original sources are in German, and the translations are mine.

d.velop is a German software company focused on document management and document-based processes. It has had a busy 2026. Here are the announcements most relevant to SAP and IT leaders in UK manufacturing.

## 1. A place in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Document Management

On 19 May 2026, d.velop announced that it had been named a "Challenger" in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Document Management, which d.velop says includes 16 vendors ([d.velop press release, in German](https://www.d-velop.de/presse/d-velop-im-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-document-management-2026)). The release cites the report as Gartner, Magic Quadrant for Document Management, Tim Nelms et al., 28 April 2026.

In the same release, d.velop says it sees the placement as confirmation of its API and its integration with third-party applications, naming SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Infor, Salesforce and Sage. It describes itself as a European group with more than 1,000 employees that has been majority family-owned for 34 years.

As always with analyst reports, Gartner's own disclaimer applies: Gartner does not endorse any vendor, product or service in its research. **I have not read the full Gartner report,** which d.velop says is available on request, so I cannot comment on Gartner's detailed assessment.

## 2. A strategic partnership for content migration

On 21 May 2026, d.velop and fme AG announced a strategic partnership for ECM content migrations ([d.velop press release, in German](https://www.d-velop.de/presse/d-velop-ag-und-fme-ag-schliessen-strategische-partnerschaft)). According to the announcement:

- The aim is to support d.velop customers migrating from existing legacy systems and third-party platforms to d.velop documents.
- fme brings its migration methodology and its own product, migration-center, to move content, metadata and document structures into the target environment.
- The two companies plan to support customers through every main phase of a migration project, from analysing the existing landscape through planning, execution and quality assurance to go-live.

Nico Bäumer, d.velop's board member and CTO, is quoted as saying: "In migration projects in particular, procedural certainty, traceability and transparency are decisive in keeping risks manageable" (my translation). The German trade site ECMGUIDE also [reported the partnership](https://www.ecmguide.de/news/einfacher-migrieren-d-velop-kooperiert-mit-fme/) on 22 May 2026.

**Why it matters for SAP teams:** migrating historical documents is often the hardest part of changing an archive. A named migration partner and method is worth asking about whichever archive platform you are evaluating.

## 3. d.velop AI, launched at d.velop SUMMIT 2026

At its SUMMIT in Düsseldorf from 9 to 11 June 2026, which d.velop says drew more than 2,000 attendees, the company launched d.velop AI ([d.velop press release, in German](https://www.d-velop.de/presse/souveraene-ki-statt-blackbox-d-velop-launcht-d-velop-ai)). d.velop describes it as a framework that brings its AI services together across five layers:

- an open infrastructure with free choice of AI model
- a "Trust Layer" covering permissions, data quality and protection against hallucinations
- the d.velop Agent Center, which orchestrates AI processes and is designed to make decisions transparent and auditable
- autonomous agents that carry out tasks
- ready-to-use "AI Experiences" for information extraction, knowledge search and automation

The release positions this for organisations that need transparency and auditability, particularly in regulated sectors. **I have not verified** which of these capabilities are generally available today, or for which deployment models.

## 4. SAP Joule and d.velop pilot

In October 2026, d.velop described on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/d-velop_ai-connects-process-and-document-knowledge-activity-7515695940444422145-DC6L) an integration between SAP Joule and its own assistant, d.velop pilot. d.velop says the integration connects SAP S/4HANA with d.velop documents, so a question such as "Which contracts are due to expire soon and which purchase orders are affected?" can be answered from one place, including the related documents, deadlines and statuses.

**I have not been able to confirm** the availability, licensing or deployment requirements of this integration. If it interests you, ask for those details directly.

## 5. SAP integration for private cloud and on-premise

On its [SAP S/4HANA Cloud page](https://www.d-velop.de/software/sap/s-4hana-cloud) (in German), d.velop states that its ArchiveLink services have been modernised and certified by SAP. It also says that, under its "One Code Multi Delivery" approach, an on-premise variant remains available to connect S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition and S/4HANA on-premise systems, and that use of the SAP ArchiveLink standard "remains fully supported for private cloud and on-premise scenarios."

For manufacturers on ECC today and moving to S/4HANA on private cloud or on-premise, that continuity of the ArchiveLink standard is a practical point to check with any archive vendor.

## What to take from this

*This section is my own view.*

Three themes run through d.velop's year: integration with business systems such as SAP, help with migrating content from older platforms, and AI that is designed to be auditable. For SAP leaders, the useful questions are the same ones you would ask any archive provider:

- How does it connect to my target S/4HANA landscape?
- How would my historical documents be migrated, and who would do it?
- Which AI features are available now, and which are still on the roadmap?
