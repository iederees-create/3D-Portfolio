## Short Video Script (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
**Hook:** Stop making silent tutorials for your digital products. 
**Visual:** Show a screen recording of a complex software interface with no audio. 
**Body:** When your customers buy your product and watch a silent tutorial, they are just trying to guess what your mouse is doing. It's frustrating. You need narration. The golden rule is: the video shows them HOW, the audio tells them WHY. Don't say "click the blue button." Say "Click export to send the report." Write the script first. If you don't want to talk, use an AI voice generator. Drop the audio in your editor, and then record your screen while listening to the audio so your mouse movements match perfectly.
