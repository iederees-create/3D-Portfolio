### Video Script (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
**Hook:** What happens to your customer's data when your automation scenario crashes? 
**Body:** If you aren't careful, that data gets lost forever in the void. But if you use Make, you can build a safety net. Go to your scenario settings and turn on "Incomplete Executions." Now, if a module fails, Make will save that data bundle. Better yet, right-click the module that might fail, add an error handler, and choose "Break." This tells Make to stop the scenario, save the data, and alert you to fix it.
