## Short Video Script (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
**Hook:** Stop sending links to your portfolio. It is costing you clients.
**Visual:** Show an email with just a link. Shake head. Then show an email with an embedded video thumbnail. Nod approvingly.
**Body:** When a client looks at your website, they just see pretty colors. They don't see the complex problem you solved, or the strategy behind the layout. You have to tell them. Record your screen walking through the site, and explain *why* you did what you did. Keep it under 2 minutes. If you hate your voice or don't have a good microphone, just write the script and use an AI voice generator to read it. It sounds completely professional.
