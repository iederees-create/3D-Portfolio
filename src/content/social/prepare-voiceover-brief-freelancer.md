# Social Pack: Prepare a Voiceover Brief a Freelancer Can Actually Use

## LinkedIn
"Just make it sound professional and energetic." 🗣️

That is the absolute worst brief you can give to a voiceover artist (or an AI voice generator). Without context, you will get a generic, corporate-sounding read that puts your audience to sleep. 

If you want a great voiceover, you need a 4-part brief:
1️⃣ Persona (Who is speaking?)
2️⃣ Pacing (How fast?)
3️⃣ Audience (Who is listening?)
4️⃣ Emotion (Pick 2 adjectives)

## X (Twitter)
