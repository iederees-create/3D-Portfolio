# Social Pack: Turn One Blog Post into Three Short Video Scripts

## LinkedIn
You just spent 4 hours writing a 1,500-word blog post. ✍️

The hard truth? Half your audience will never read it because they only consume short-form video. 

Don't let that content go to waste. You can easily turn one blog post into 3 distinct, high-performing videos. 

Here is the formula:
1️⃣ Video 1: Talk about the Core Problem (Intro).
2️⃣ Video 2: Teach the Best Tip (Body paragraph).
3️⃣ Video 3: Share the Controversial Take (Conclusion/Opinion).

Keep each script to 150 words (60 seconds). Generate the voiceover with AI, throw on some auto-captions and b-roll, and you're done.

## Short Video Script (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
**Hook:** You are wasting your best content, and here is how to fix it right now.
**Visual:** Pointing to a laptop showing a long, text-heavy blog post. 
**Body:** You just spent hours writing a blog post, but half your audience only watches video. Stop summarizing the whole post in one video—it's too much info. Instead, make three videos. Video one: Talk about the biggest problem your article solves. Video two: Share the single most actionable tip from the article. Video three: Share your most controversial opinion from the article. Keep each script to exactly 150 words. That's a 60-second video. Generate the voice with AI, add some b-roll, and boom. Three pieces of content from one article.
