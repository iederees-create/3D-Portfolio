Human Voice Actor vs. AI Voiceover 🥊

Which one should you choose for your next video project? The answer is: It depends on the project. 

Use AI when:
✅ You need high-volume content (daily Shorts/TikToks).
✅ You need to localize a video into 15 languages by tomorrow. 
✅ You are A/B testing ad variations and need to tweak scripts instantly. 

Hire a Human when:
✅ You are telling a highly emotional, vulnerable story. 
✅ You need complex character acting or comedic timing.
✅ It's a flagship, high-budget brand anthem.
