2027 monthly fees

  • Half day, 7:00 AM - 12:30 PM: R2,350 per month
  • Half day, 7:00 AM - 2:00 PM: R2,550 per month
  • Full day: R3,350 per month. Monday-Thursday, 7:00 AM - 5:30 PM; Friday, 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM.

Payment terms

  • Fees are payable over 12 months.
  • Overdue fees incur a R100 fine under the school's existing policy.
  • Fees remain payable when a child is absent.
  • The school operates normally during the March, June and September school holidays; full fees remain payable.
  • Existing debit-order arrangements continue. Confirm the payment date and instructions directly with the school. No online school-fee payments are collected on this website.
