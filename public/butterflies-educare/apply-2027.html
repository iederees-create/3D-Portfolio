Code of Conduct

Butterflies Educare expects children to treat others kindly, avoid bullying and aggression, respect classmates and teachers, follow guidance and learn peaceful conflict resolution. The school's policy provides for discussions, parental support and further action when difficulties persist.

Fees & Payment Policy

According to the policy supplied by the school: fees are due over 12 months; overdue fees incur a R100 fine; fees remain payable when a child is absent; and the school operates normally during the March, June and September school holidays with full fees due. Fee amounts and payment instructions must be confirmed by the school.

Privacy and declaration

Your application is visible only to your account and authorised school staff. Read the data-handling information. The school must provide its approved POPIA notice and retention information before broad enrolment.
