A mobile home for your school community, with butterfly branding, a fixed bottom navigation bar and quick family shortcuts. Manage profiles, children, classes, events and school messages in native app screens.

Version 0.2.0 · Test release · Android 8 or newer · Free

  1. If you have the earlier 0.1.0 test app, remove it first. Your account and saved school records stay online. Download the new APK, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app, then tap Install. You can turn that permission off afterwards.
  3. Open Butterflies Educare and sign in with your existing school email and password.

New here? Create one account in the app or on the website. Your school records are shared between both.

This first test version needs an internet connection. Full 2027 application entry, policy documents and advanced school administration open on the school website. Please test your account before sharing the app widely.

Use the home-screen web app instead

This optional web app uses your browser. It is separate from the Android APK above.

Android: Open this page in Chrome. Tap Add web app to home screen when available, or use the browser menu → Install app / Add to Home screen.

iPhone or iPad: Open in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if shown, then Add.

Computer: Open in Chrome or Edge and use the install icon in the address bar when available.

If you opened this link inside WhatsApp, open it in your device’s browser first.

One account, wherever you sign in

The app and website use the same school system. Your existing email, password, profile and registrations work in both. You may need to sign in again after installing. Staff permissions still require school approval.

Internet access is needed for sign-in, current information and submitting changes. Installation is free. The Android test APK is downloaded directly from this school website. The optional web app installs through your browser. Neither is an App Store or Google Play listing.
