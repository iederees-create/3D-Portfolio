Website information

Privacy & data handling

This is an interim technical information page, not a final school-approved privacy policy. Butterflies Educare must approve its full privacy notice, official contact details, information officer arrangements and retention periods before the parent network is promoted broadly. Do not submit sensitive information about children through the current forms.

What is collected

When you create a parent or community account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and sign-in credentials. If you complete a parent profile, the network stores the display name, optional contact number, chosen communication preference and a record of your privacy acknowledgement. You can submit general enrolment-information enquiries, private messages to staff, event responses, sponsorship enquiries and volunteering interests.

Enrolment enquiries currently ask only about an age range and preferred starting period. They are not official applications, and the forms must not be used to submit a child's name, medical details, ID number, photograph or other sensitive information.

Who can see it

Parents can view their own profile and submitted activity using their signed-in account. Authorised school staff, when assigned, can review the enquiries and contact details needed to respond. Public visitors can read only school information, projects, events and announcements marked for publication. The database enforces role-based access rules.

Where information is processed

The website is served through GitHub Pages. Account identity and school-network records use an existing shared Supabase project; the school's records are held in dedicated Butterflies Educare tables. The existing Render service supplies read-only published school content. These external providers may process information outside South Africa. The school must approve the final data-processing and cross-border arrangements.

Information kept in your browser

The optional family morning checklist and interface preferences may be kept in your browser's local storage. Supabase also stores authentication-session information in your browser to keep you signed in. Sponsorship introduction drafts are generated locally and are not sent to the school unless you choose to copy or send them through another channel.

Account choices and correction requests

You can update your contact profile in the parent network. There is not yet a self-service account deletion or personal-data export feature. The school must establish a verified contact channel for access, correction, deletion and privacy requests before public onboarding is completed.

School communication and payments

Publishing school information, replying to private messages and confirming arrangements are responsibilities of authorised school staff. School fees, fundraising payments and collection of children's sensitive data are not enabled on this website.

Next steps

Before registering, please read this information and use only the general fields provided. The school will publish an approved policy, contact channel and retention terms when these are confirmed. The South African Information Regulator provides additional information about the Protection of Personal Information Act.
