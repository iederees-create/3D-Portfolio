Privacy & data handling

This is an interim technical information page, not a final school-approved privacy policy. The 2027 application form now collects children's registration details and may include optional health information. Butterflies Educare must approve its full privacy notice, official contact details, information officer arrangements, lawful basis and retention periods before inviting families to submit real children's information.

What is collected

When you create a parent or community account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and sign-in credentials. If you complete a parent profile, the network stores the display name, optional contact number, chosen communication preference and a record of your privacy acknowledgement. You can submit general enrolment-information enquiries, private messages to staff, event responses, sponsorship enquiries and volunteering interests. Parents may also register interest in an existing weekly class by supplying their class choice, name, email, optional phone number, age group and question. These enquiries are not confirmed class registrations.

General enrolment enquiries continue to ask only about an age range and preferred starting period. Separately, the private 2027 application form can collect a child's name, date of birth, address, school grade, parent and guardian contact details, emergency contact information and, only if the parent chooses to provide it with separate consent, allergies, medical conditions, medication and doctor contact information. The application also records acknowledgements of the Code of Conduct, Fees & Payment Policy and privacy information. No identification-document or photograph uploads are supported.

Who can see it

Parents can view their own profile, enquiries and applications using their signed-in account. Verified school administrators can review 2027 applications, including optional health details submitted with the parent's consent. Authorised staff can review enquiries and contact details within their assigned role. Public visitors can read only approved published school information. Database row-level security rules restrict private application records and class registration enquiries to the submitting parent and authorised school administrators.

Where information is processed

The website is served through GitHub Pages. Account identity and school-network records use an existing shared Supabase project; the school's records are held in dedicated Butterflies Educare tables. The existing Render service supplies read-only published school content. These external providers may process information outside South Africa. The school must approve the final data-processing and cross-border arrangements.

Information kept in your browser

The optional family morning checklist and interface preferences may be kept in your browser's local storage. Supabase also stores authentication-session information in your browser to keep you signed in. Sponsorship introduction drafts are generated locally and are not sent to the school unless you choose to copy or send them through another channel.

Account choices and correction requests

You can update your contact profile in the parent network. There is not yet a self-service account deletion or personal-data export feature. The school must establish a verified contact channel for access, correction, deletion and privacy requests before public onboarding is completed.

School communication and payments

Publishing school information, replying to private messages and confirming arrangements are responsibilities of authorised school staff. School fees and fundraising payments are not enabled on this website. The new private 2027 application collects registration details, with optional health details controlled by separate consent. A submitted application is a request for admission and does not guarantee a place.

Next steps

Before submitting an application about a child, review this information and ensure you are authorised to act as the child's guardian. The school must publish its approved privacy policy, contact channel and retention terms before broad public enrolment. The South African Information Regulator provides additional information about the Protection of Personal Information Act.
