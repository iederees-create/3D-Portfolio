Parents & guardians

  1. Open the parent portal and select Create account. Enter your name, email and a password of your choice.
  2. Check your inbox, including Spam or Junk, and click the confirmation link.
  3. Return to the portal and sign in. Complete your contact profile and privacy acknowledgement.
  4. Add your child’s basic profile if appropriate. The school will verify and link its enrolment register to your guardian account.
  5. Explore classes, RSVP for events, offer fundraising support and send messages to the school.

Already enrolled at Butterflies? Creating an account does not require a new admission application. Complete one only if the school asks you to. New applicants can use the separate 2027 application form; applications are reviewed before a place is offered.

Class providers & community presenters

  1. Create and verify your community account, then complete your contact profile. You can use an existing parent account.
  2. Open Offer a class on the school calendar.
  3. Describe your activity, suitable ages, experience, fees, equipment and space needs.
  4. Propose the first session, final date, number and length of sessions, recurring schedule and alternative availability.
  5. Submit your proposal. Return to the same page to read its status and any school reply.

A proposal does not confirm a booking or reserve a slot. The school will agree safeguarding checks, supervision, fees and dates before publishing the class.

Teachers & school staff

Create and verify your account, save your contact profile and tell the principal which school duties you need to manage. Staff permissions are approved separately. Creating an account does not automatically grant access to school or family records.
