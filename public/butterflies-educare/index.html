Good to know

Questions from families.

How do I enquire about enrolling my child?

You can create an account in the Parent & Community Network to request enrolment information. 2027 applications are open. Complete the online application or use the paper form. Read the confirmed 2027 fees and payment policy. The school reviews each application before confirming a place.

Can I make school payments here?

No online school-fee or donation payments are collected here. Existing debit-order arrangements continue. Read the 2027 fee policy and contact the school for payment instructions.

Where can I find school dates and announcements?

The News & Happenings section will show information approved and published by the school.

Can parents share photos and information?

Any future photo gallery or private parent features will be subject to school approval, relevant consent and privacy safeguards.
