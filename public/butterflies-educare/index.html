Good to know

Questions from families.

How do I enquire about enrolling my child?

You can create an account in the Parent & Community Network to request enrolment information. Official eligibility, places and fees are still being confirmed. An enquiry is not an application.

Can I make school payments here?

Not yet. Online fees or donations will only be enabled after the school confirms its payment provider, amounts and processes.

Where can I find school dates and announcements?

The News & Happenings section will show information approved and published by the school.

Can parents share photos and information?

Any future photo gallery or private parent features will be subject to school approval, relevant consent and privacy safeguards.
