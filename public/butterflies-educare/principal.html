Help everyone move online

  1. Add the current school register and check guardian email addresses.
  2. Share the parent invitation through your usual school channel.
  3. Ask parents to verify their email and save their contact profile.
  4. Check identities and link each guardian to their learner record.
  5. Help families who need assistance at school, then use the portal for announcements and events.
