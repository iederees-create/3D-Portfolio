What we do

Crystal clear pools, quality service, lasting results

From a brand new installation to a filter service, Xceed Pools covers the full range of pool work for homes across the Western Cape.

New pool installations

Planning a new pool at home? We install new swimming pools and can talk you through the next steps.

Fibrelining

A fresh fibre lining gives an older pool a clean, watertight finish and a new lease of life.

Pool painting, mosaics, tiling and resurfacing

We paint pools, install mosaics, and help with tiling and resurfacing, so the finish looks sharp and lasts through the Cape seasons.

Renovations and restorations

Tired, empty or leaking pools can be restored and renovated so they are ready to use again.

Pumps, filters and sand changes

We install and replace pumps and filters, clean cartridge filters, change sand, and can upgrade to filter glass.

Solar panels and heat pumps

Keep the water warmer for longer with solar panels or a heat pump. We supply and install ITS and Alliance inverters for all pool sizes.

Cleaning and maintenance

Regular pool cleaning plus major and minor repairs, so your pool stays in shape all year.

Plumbing, lighting and electricals

We attend to pool plumbing, lighting and electrical repairs, including salt chlorinator installation.

Water treatment

Water treatment and chemistry work to help keep the pool clear, healthy and ready to swim.
